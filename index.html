Contact us

Found a bug, have a question or an idea? Email redpandacake@gmail.com. For a bug, please include your device model, its iOS version and what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device, in up to three save files. Deleting the game deletes your saves, so keep it installed to keep your progress.

How do I delete a save file?

On the title screen, tap the trash can on that save, then confirm. This can't be undone.

Do adventures keep going when I close the game?

Yes. Adventures play out in real time. To hear when a party gets back, turn on Adventure Notifications or Live Activity in the game's Settings, under Adventures.

How do I play on iPhone Duo?

Any way you hold it. Folded, the whole game plays on the outer screen. Open it held wide and your pets' home and your adventures sit side by side like an open book; hold it tall for one page above the other. Battles keep running while you fold, open or turn it.

Can I turn the sound down?

Yes. In the game's Settings, under Sound, you can turn interface sounds and battle sounds on or off. The game also follows your device's silent switch.
