Privacy Policy

Sword & Paw, by Redpandacake. Effective October 22, 2026.

Sword & Paw doesn't collect any information about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the game doesn't share anything with anyone.

Your game progress

Your save files are stored only on your device, inside the game. They never leave it, except as part of your device's own backups (iCloud or a computer), which Apple's privacy policy covers. Deleting the game deletes your saves.

Notifications and Live Activities

Adventure notifications and the Lock Screen Live Activity are optional and off until you turn them on in the game's Settings. They're created on your device; no server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere.

Purchases

Apple handles every purchase through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
